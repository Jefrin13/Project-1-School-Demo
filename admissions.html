How it works

Four steps to a new school year.

From enquiry to enrolment — here's exactly what the process looks like, start to finish.

01

Submit enquiry

Fill out the form below or call our admissions desk with your child's details.

02

Campus visit

Take a guided tour of the campus and meet the faculty for your child's grade.

03

Entrance assessment

A simple, friendly assessment to understand your child's current learning level.

04

Confirm admission

Complete document submission and fee payment to secure your child's seat.
