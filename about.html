Our story

A school that began with 120 children and a borrowed building.

Your School was founded in 1995 by a small group of teachers who believed education had become too transactional. They wanted to build a place where character was taken as seriously as calculus, and where every child — regardless of background — could find their own kind of brilliance.

Three decades later, that founding belief still shapes everything we do. From our youngest pre-kindergarteners learning the alphabet through stories, to our Class 12 students designing science experiments — we still measure success by the human a child becomes, not just the marks they score.

Today over 3,000 students walk through our gates each morning, taught by 75 educators who chose this school not because of pay, but because of purpose.
